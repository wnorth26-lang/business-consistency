# 6–8 Week Launch Scorecard

Evidence hierarchy: **payment > repeat production use > configured first use > install > beta signup > issue/discussion > star > page view**.

Track weekly: stars/forks, install/clone signals, repeat users, use-case issues, connector requests, Discussions, beta signups, pilots and paying users.

Do not judge the experiment by stars alone. Freeze numerical kill thresholds only after benchmarking comparable young infrastructure repos.
